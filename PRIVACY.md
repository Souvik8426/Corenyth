# Corenyth Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Corenyth is an offline, on-device task assistant. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your data stays on your phone.**

## What Corenyth stores

Your chats, tasks, reminders and memories are saved in a database on your device. The language model also runs on your device. Corenyth does not have the Android `INTERNET` permission, so the app cannot send this data anywhere.

## What Corenyth does not do

- It does not collect, transmit or sell personal data.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
- It does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show task reminders you set.
- **Exact alarms:** so a reminder fires at the time you chose for a task.
- **Run at start-up:** to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.

## Files you choose

When you import a model file or a backup, you pick the file with Android's file picker and Corenyth copies it into its own storage. Backups you export are created only when you ask, and leave the app only if you share them yourself.

## Feedback and downloads

"Send feedback" opens your own mail app with a draft addressed to the developer; nothing is sent until you press send. The model download button opens your browser, which is governed by that website's own policy.

## Children

Corenyth is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can also delete chats, tasks and memories inside the app.
